\## Beyond 1.0



The long-term vision is for OpenSec Atlas to become shared infrastructure for security knowledge around emerging technologies.



New technologies should be able to enter the Atlas without requiring the project architecture to be redesigned.



The roadmap will evolve with the technology and the community.
