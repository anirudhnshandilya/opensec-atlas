\# Contributing to OpenSec Atlas



Thank you for contributing to OpenSec Atlas.



The project is designed to become a community-maintained security knowledge and research ecosystem for emerging technologies.



\## What can you contribute?



\- Technologies

\- Threats

\- Attacks

\- Defenses

\- Detections

\- Security labs

\- Benchmarks

\- Research reproductions

\- Datasets

\- Experiments

\- Documentation

\- Tooling



\## Before contributing



1\. Search existing issues and pull requests.

2\. Check whether the contribution already exists.

3\. For significant work, open an issue first.

4\. Keep contributions focused.

5\. Provide evidence and references where possible.



\## Evidence and attribution



Clearly distinguish between:



\- Verified findings

\- Reproduced research

\- Experimental observations

\- Hypotheses

\- Assumptions

\- Open questions



Do not present speculation as established fact.



Credit original researchers, projects, datasets, tools, and sources.



\## Security research



Only perform security testing against systems you are authorized to test.



Use controlled and isolated environments for demonstrations whenever possible.



Follow responsible disclosure requirements when working with vulnerabilities affecting third-party projects.



\## Pull requests



A pull request should:



\- Explain what changed.

\- Explain why it changed.

\- Include relevant evidence or references.

\- Include validation where appropriate.

\- Avoid unrelated changes.

\- Preserve reproducibility.



\## Research contributions



Research contributions should provide, where applicable:



\- Research question

\- Threat model

\- Environment

\- Dependencies

\- Reproduction steps

\- Experimental configuration

\- Expected results

\- Observed results

\- Limitations

\- References



\## Review



Contributions may be reviewed for:



\- Technical accuracy

\- Reproducibility

\- Evidence

\- Clarity

\- Security impact

\- Licensing

\- Responsible disclosure



OpenSec Atlas is intended to be built collaboratively. Constructive review is part of the project.
